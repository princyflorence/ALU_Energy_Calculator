SUBMISSION PACKAGE
==================
Project : Design and Verification of a 4-Bit ALU-Based Smart Energy Consumption Calculator Using Verilog HDL
Author  : princyflorence
Date    : October 2026

Contents of this repository follow a complete VLSI design flow:

1. rtl/          - Synthesizable RTL (Verilog)
2. tb/           - Testbench
3. sim/          - Simulation outputs (VCD)
4. synthesis/    - Yosys synthesis reports & netlists
5. sta/          - Multi-corner Static Timing Analysis (TT/SS/FF)
6. physical/     - Placement, routing, DRC, antenna, timing reports
7. constraints/  - SDC timing constraints
8. waveforms/    - Waveform captures
9. docs/         - Design notes
10. signoff_checklist.md - Final sign-off checklist

All major stages of the VLSI flow have been completed and documented.
